- Locked weapon is the center triple-coil design, shortened for two-hand mounting.
- Armor, chip and pet each have a unique equipment silhouette and remain in the shared set language.
- Free automatic cannon, handheld Tesla coil and flamethrower retain different gameplay functions and remain visually below the legendary weapon.

- Phone-scale proof keeps heroes at roughly 14-17 percent of viewport height.

Still not runtime-ready:

- These are review composites, not transparent production sprites.

- UI texture slicing, ThemeManager, premium data and StoreKit remain Phase 1B+ work.
